# Backlog

The backlog is the full task list for a project, in priority order. The board shows a snapshot of it in columns; the backlog is the source of truth.

## Working with the backlog

- **Open the backlog** from the project sidebar.
- **Filter** by labels, assignee, status, or search term to focus on a slice of work.
- **New task** (`n`) adds a card here; it appears on the board in the first column.
- **Edit** any task's title, description, labels, assignee, and due date from its card.

## Relationship to the board

Moving a card between board columns updates its backlog status. Labeling a task on the board is reflected here. The two views are the same underlying data, rendered differently.
